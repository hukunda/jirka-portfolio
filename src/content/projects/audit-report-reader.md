## Problem space

Audit workflows combine sensitivity, heavy documents, and ambiguous collaboration timing. The platform had to feel organised without implying false certainty — and had to work for roles with very different mental models of “done”.

## Key decisions & trade‑offs

- Human‑centred structure for navigation and review flows (not a generic document portal skin)
- Regular **user testing** loops (including remote tooling) to catch friction early
- Dedicated microcopy and Help Centre work so failures didn’t dump users into dead ends

## Outcome

A calmer review experience with clearer handoffs between parties — fewer support escalations driven by “where do I go next?” ambiguity, and UX artefacts that engineering could implement without constant reinterpretation.
