## Problem space

IAM programmes fail as often from operational friction as from technology. Users needed clarity on who could access what, why approvals existed, and where processes stalled — without exposing more risk than the organisation could govern.

## Key decisions & trade‑offs

- Early journey analysis to target automation where it removed repetitive failure modes (not where it looked futuristic)
- MVP sequencing: prove value with **clickable prototypes**, then expand features once feedback stabilised
- Later dashboards for executive visibility and privacy‑sensitive improvements once baseline workflows held together

## Outcome

Less manual chasing across teams, clearer status visibility for stakeholders, and UX deliverables that matched how IAM programmes actually land in large organisations — incremental, constrained, and audit‑conscious.
