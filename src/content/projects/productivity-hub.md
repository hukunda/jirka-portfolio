## Problem space

What began as “see our portfolio of work” kept absorbing adjacent needs: milestones, ownership, dependencies, and reporting expectations from different sponsors. The UX risk was a growing surface that looked capable but felt fragmented for day‑to‑day use.

## Key decisions & trade‑offs

- Research and iterative prototyping before committing to a single PM paradigm
- **Card sorting** and **tree testing** to stabilise navigation as labels and mental models diverged across user groups
- Deliberate boundaries between “portfolio visibility” and “operational control” so the product didn’t pretend to be full enterprise PPM without the backend reality

## Outcome

A tool that could mature with the organisation — clearer paths through status and ownership, fewer dead ends as features accrued, and UX decisions that remained explainable when the roadmap expanded.
