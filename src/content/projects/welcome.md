## SALT (State and Local Tax)

SALT stands for State and Local Tax. SALT teams handle tax compliance for businesses across US states and cities: filing the correct returns, meeting local obligations, and helping optimise strategies through state-specific credits, incentives, and planning opportunities.

The **SALT TRACK** initiative focuses on simplifying and automating how state tax returns are prepared for Financial Services (FS) clients.

## What is TRACK?

**TRACK** (Tax Reporting and Consulting for subchapter K) is an end-to-end tax reporting system used to prepare partnership K-1s. It ingests financial information, allocates and tiers taxable income through multi-tier partnership structures, produces reports and forms, and bridges into e-file software. TRACK is one of the primary compliance tools for the FS Tax practice.

## Why this work exists

The driver is **efficiency through end-to-end automation and standardisation**, with stronger diagnostics and support for more complex structures — including Fund of Funds (FoF) and Private Equity (PE).

## Vision for TRACK

- **Automation & standardisation:** End-to-end automation and standardisation of the state return preparation process.
- **Standardised profiles:** Profiles that drive consistent presentation throughout the flow.
- **Revamped reporting & UI:** Highlight deviations from standardised processes, improve the experience, simplify the interface, and embed best practices for everyday use.
- **FoF / PE focus:** Strong emphasis on Fund of Funds and Private Equity in the first wave.

**Fund of Funds (FoF)** describes a fund that invests in a collection of other funds rather than directly in stocks or bonds — diversification across multiple underlying funds.

**Private Equity (PE)** covers investors and firms that invest in private companies or take public companies private, improve value, and seek an exit.

## Reimagining the TRACK user experience

- Drive **standardisation of calculations and return preparation**
- **Revamp reporting** to support diagnostics and review
- **Prioritise FoF and PE** client scenarios in scope and design decisions
