## Problem space

The catalogue was expanding faster than people could build a mental model of it. Users needed to discover relevant AI capabilities quickly, compare options without jargon overload, and trust that what they selected matched their situation — not a generic “AI menu”.

## Key decisions & trade‑offs

- Market and landscape scan to frame how teams actually look for tools (not only how we preferred to organise the backlog)
- Workshops and research inputs to stress‑test navigation schemes before committing build cost
- Strong emphasis on **smart search** and scannable structure so reach improved without turning the surface into a feature list
- Iterative prototyping so navigation changes could be validated before locking technical integration

## Outcome

Better orientation inside a crowded toolset: clearer paths to the right AI capability, less dead‑end browsing, and a UX stance that treated “availability” as partly a discovery problem — not only a catalogue problem.
