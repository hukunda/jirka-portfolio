## Problem space

Requirements were often incomplete, priorities competing, and timelines ambitious. Design decisions needed to survive beyond initial delivery and still make sense to teams joining later.

## Key decisions & trade‑offs

- Framing the problem space early to avoid solving symptoms instead of causes
- Translating business goals and technical constraints into clear UX principles
- Facilitating workshops to align stakeholders before design diverged too far
- Designing for consistency, maintainability, and realistic implementation
- Regular reviews focused on clarity and trade‑offs rather than personal taste

## Outcome

Products that were understandable, defensible, and easier to evolve over time, even as teams and priorities changed.
