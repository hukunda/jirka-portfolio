## Problem space

Every team had a valid reason to tweak a chart or filter set — and the result was a growing library of one-off pages that didn’t line up in reviews. The UX problem was not “prettier dashboards”, but **comparable, explainable views** when executives and operators asked the same question in different rooms.

## Key decisions & trade‑offs

- **Template tiers** — standard shells for common review types, with explicit extension points instead of free-form edits
- **Measure discipline** — pairing visuals with definitions people could trace back to source conversations
- **Navigation patterns** — repeatable paths from summary to supporting tables without losing context at each drill step

## Outcome

Reporting that could scale with the organisation: fewer ambiguous comparisons in steering forums, faster onboarding for new report consumers, and design choices that stayed legible when datasets and refreshes evolved behind the scenes.
