## Problem space

AML monitoring surfaces are inherently heavy: huge volumes, ambiguous signals, and users who must act fast without misunderstanding model output. The business needed cost‑effective monitoring without UX that hid risk behind complexity — or trained people to trust automation blindly.

## Key decisions & trade‑offs

- Double‑diamond style framing to align business need, user reality, and technical feasibility before UI polish
- Design from wireframes through **complete prototype** so flows could be challenged end‑to‑end early
- Patterns that supported investigation workflows — not only dashboard aesthetics — including readability of alerts and drill‑down paths
- Continuous tightening so users could **navigate complicated data** without losing the thread of what the system believed was happening

## Outcome

A monitoring experience that matched operational reality: easier orientation through complicated data, clearer paths from signal to context, and UX decisions that supported both financial impact and defensible human judgement.
