## Problem space

Teams were shipping faster than patterns stayed coherent. Developers needed implementable components; designers needed a shared language; users paid the price when every product reinvented tables, forms, and navigation differently.

## Key decisions & trade‑offs

- Double‑diamond discipline paired with **continuous research and usability feedback** — not a one‑shot “DS workshop”
- Investment in **Figma libraries** so design intent survived implementation
- Close collaboration with engineering on component evolution — including migrations that improved baseline quality across applications
- Explicit trade‑offs: fewer bespoke hero flows, more repeatable patterns that scaled

## Outcome

A system teams could actually adopt: faster builds, fewer mismatches between design and code, and a credible step‑change in consistency — including migration outcomes that improved everyday UX across a large internal application landscape.
