## Problem space

Users were domain experts working under time pressure, but often without deep understanding of how AI models behaved. The risk was building tools that were powerful but hard to trust or explain in day‑to‑day work.

## Key decisions & trade‑offs

- Discovery focused on real workflows, edge cases, and failure scenarios
- Clear separation between user intent, system suggestions, and final user decisions
- UX patterns exposing model confidence and system limits without overwhelming users
- Iterative prototyping and validation focused on comprehension, not just task completion
- Ongoing alignment with engineering constraints and delivery realities

## Outcome

The resulting UX supported efficient expert workflows while making AI behaviour understandable and reducing blind trust in automated outputs.
