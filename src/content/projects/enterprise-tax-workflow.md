## Problem space

State and local tax work for Financial Services clients sits next to heavy partnership tax reporting: large volumes of structured financial data, tiered allocations, and tight filing deadlines. Teams needed **efficiency through end‑to‑end automation and standardisation**, stronger **diagnostics** when something diverges from the expected path, and explicit support for **more complex structures** — especially Fund of Funds (FoF) and Private Equity (PE) — without turning the UI into a bespoke tool per client.

## Key decisions & trade‑offs

- **Automation & standardisation** across the state return preparation journey — fewer manual handoffs, clearer sequencing from intake through review
- **Standardised profiles** so presentation stays consistent across steps and roles don’t invent parallel interpretations of the same entity
- **Reporting & interface** oriented to **exceptions and deviations** from the standard process — surfacing what needs attention instead of burying it in uniform grids
- **FoF / PE focus** in early releases — accepting narrower scope first so scenarios that break simpler flows get real design attention rather than late‑stage rework
- Plain‑language alignment with **explainability and audit trails**: usability tied to trust in regulated work, not only task completion speed

## Outcome

A clearer path toward scalable state return preparation UX — better diagnostics for review, a calmer progression through standardized calculation and prep steps, and design choices that stay legible when structures, tiers, and filing rules get harder rather than easier.
