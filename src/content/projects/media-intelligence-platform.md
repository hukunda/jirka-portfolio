## Problem space

Stakeholders needed a coherent view across channels and sources where definitions rarely lined up perfectly. The UX challenge wasn’t “more charts”, but trustworthy orientation: knowing what could be compared, what was incomplete, and where to drill without losing context.

## Key decisions & trade‑offs

- Design‑thinking workshops to align sprint topics with measurable decisions (not generic discovery theatre)
- Strong emphasis on **filtering logic** and visual hierarchy so dense views stayed navigable
- Migration discipline to **shared design-system components** — fewer bespoke widgets, more repeatable patterns across dashboards

## Outcome

Improved consistency in navigation and visualisation logic; fewer “similar but different” screens; and regular design–tech touchpoints that reduced rework from mismatched assumptions.
