## Problem space

AI initiatives were moving faster than teams’ ability to reason about risk, responsibility, and impact. The challenge wasn’t only usability, but helping different roles understand *what the system does*, *why it behaves the way it does*, and *where human responsibility starts and ends*.

## Key decisions & trade‑offs

- Early discovery with diverse stakeholders to align on goals, constraints, and non‑negotiables
- Structuring an abstract problem into understandable user goals and mental models
- Iterative concept design balancing governance requirements with day‑to‑day usability
- Deliberate focus on clarity and transparency instead of feature completeness
- Continuous validation with representative users and reality checks with delivery and legal teams

## Outcome

The UX helped teams reason about AI systems more confidently, discuss risks explicitly, and work responsibly without turning the tool into a compliance obstacle.
